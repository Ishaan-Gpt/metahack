## The vertical before/after post (40 s, 1080x1920)

**What it is for the viewer:** in two seconds they see a flat phone take turn into something that looks paid for, and they
want to try it on their own.

- **The take:** Ishaan's own raw phone take, 20-30 s, one he owns outright. (The studio's sample is CC BY-SA 4.0, which would
  make the post share-alike and needs a credit line; his own take avoids both.)
- **Sound:** the edit's own mix (his voice, the effects, the bed). One voice only, no voice-over on top.
- **Text:** inside the Shorts safe area (y 230-1480, x 70-905), never over his face; labels sit below the chin or beside the head.
- **Pace:** a visible change every 1.2-2 s, the hook inside 2 s.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:02 | The edit's strongest beat (the big word behind his head) full frame, the raw take in a small inset beside his shoulder, both playing | `my raw take` (on the inset) · `Moovi's edit` |
| 0:02-0:12 | The take flips raw → edit on every sentence: about 0.6 s of the flat raw, then the edited beat, so the gap between them is felt, not explained | none |
